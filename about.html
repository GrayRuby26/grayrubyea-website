About

Hi, I'm Shannon

Founder of Gray Ruby Executive Assistants, and the person you'll be working with.

From site office to your support team

My background is in construction and project administration: financial admin, workplace health and safety, contractor coordination and travel for project teams. Whether it's a progress claim, an HR training module or a board pack, I know what good looks like.
