Questions

Frequently asked

How does pricing work?

There are no fixed packages. After a free chat about what you need, I put together a service agreement that sets out the scope, pricing and terms, so you only pay for the support you use.

Do you only work remotely?

Most work is done remotely, but I also offer on-site office days in Brisbane and surrounds, and can travel to job sites when required.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

I work with business owners and directors across a range of industries, including HR consultancies, professional services and construction. Every client gets support tailored to the way their business works, whatever the industry.

Can you work in our systems?

Yes. I work in the tools you already use, and if your systems need some tidying up, I can assist with setting up SharePoint sites, file structures, registers and templates.

Where are your clients based?

I'm based in Brisbane and support businesses remotely across Australia.
